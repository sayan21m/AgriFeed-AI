    Essential Dairy Feed Classes & Quality Standards

    Scientific guidelines based on NDDB, BIS IS 2052, and ICAR Indian animal nutrition standards

    Ration Balancing · NDDB

    Dairy Cow Protein Balance

    A lactating crossbred cow's total mixed ration requires 12–16% crude protein on dry matter. Known feeds lookup calibrated Indian tables rather than guessing.

    Target: 12–16% CP on DM
    Silage Fermentation · Flieg Score

    Pit Silage Acidification

    Good silage fermentation requires pH below 4.2. High pH with low dry matter triggers clostridial effluent spoilage and is flagged for dilution or rejection.

    Flieg Formula · Max pH 4.2
    Roughage · Mineral Advisory

    Straws & Mineral Supplementation

    Wheat & paddy straws provide bulk fibre but lack Calcium and Phosphorus. AgriFeed-AI automatically advises 50–100g Area-Specific Mineral Mixture (ASMM) daily.

    50–100g ASMM Supplement
    Adulteration Safety · BIS IS 2052

    Urea & Moisture Safety Limits

    Commercial compounded feed must never exceed 1% urea or 11% moisture. Colorimetric 4-DMAB and settling jar tests screen urea and silica contamination.

    BIS Max 1% Urea · Max 11% Moisture

    Village Kit Architecture

    Low-cost field hardware paired with scientifically honest local smartphone scoring

    Portable Hardware (ESP32)

    The physical village kit comprises a capacitive moisture probe, an industrial pH electrode, an 18-channel AS7265x optical spectral sensor, and a 4-DMAB colorimetric reaction chamber.

    • Moisture Probe: Converts Dry Matter values to as-fed intake.
    • pH Electrode: Evaluates silage pit acidification (< 4.2 lactic target).
    • 4-DMAB Reagent: Turns bright yellow if urea is adulterated above BIS 1%.
    • Local Hotspot: Connects to mobile phone via Wi-Fi SmartFeed.
